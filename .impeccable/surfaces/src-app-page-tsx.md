# Front page and project page

Scope: the front page (`/`) and the project page (`/projects/<name>`, built first for Rick). Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience and job

People who might install an Open-Breach tool. On the front page they learn what Open-Breach is and find a project. On a project page they see the project, learn why it exists, and install it.

- Primary action: Install. Second action: Join the discussion.
- Proof on hand: the projects themselves, Rick's screenshots and README, the statement that the work is done with Claude.
- Would feel wrong (Serge, 2026-11-23): heavy or dark; a generic SaaS landing page.
- Texts, landscape, releases, badge values and the team entries are placeholders in round one.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Precise, light technology standing in raw terrain. The page is a pale instrument panel laid over a coastline of black and grey stone, and the tools are shown as themselves. It refuses the start-up hero: no centred headline over a gradient, no grid of icon cards, no wall of claims.

OWN-WORLD: Mist ground that darkens towards the land. Basalt type, heavy and tight for display. Stone hairlines. Glass panels: milky, softened backdrop, thin light edge, soft offset shadow. The instrument scale, a ruled line with tick marks, is the system's divider and measure. Signal amber only on actions and small signals. Labels in spaced capitals; versions, dates and commands in mono. The landscape is layered ridges over a band of ocean with thin horizontal lines. No green, no turquoise.

STORY: The visitor understands that Open-Breach makes open tools, in the open, with Claude, with no commercial interest. They see the five projects, pick one, see it at work, read why it exists, and install it or join the discussion.

FIRST VIEWPORT: As on board B at 1440 by 960. Top left, the navigation as one rounded glass panel. Below it on the left: a small amber label, a two-line headline at about 84px, two lines of secondary text, the instrument scale, then Install (filled amber, dark label) and Join the discussion (outlined). On the right, a glass card for Rick that overlaps the ridge line. The lower 40 per cent is the landscape, edge to edge, ending in the ocean band. At 390 wide the same order in one column, the card under the buttons, the landscape below.

FORM: seed: none (pinned). "Board B and design.md are the brief. Do not pick another direction." Built code-led: the landscape is a drawn SVG stand-in by decision (design D5), and the board is a mood board, so it serves as the critique reference and not as a measured comp. Signature interaction: the instrument scale draws itself in on load, ticks in sequence. Motion grammar: short exponential ease-out, fades and small lifts only; the landscape drifts slightly against the scroll; with reduced motion, fades only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
